## Implementation capabilities to validate on a live account

These are useful services to offer, but a client engagement or a sanitized technical sample should be added before describing them as a completed client result:

- Shopify theme and Liquid customization.
- GA4 and Google Tag Manager implementation.
- Triple Whale or equivalent e-commerce dashboard setup.
- Google Ads and TikTok Ads campaign operations.
- Advanced server-side CAPI implementation beyond the observed integration label.

## Safe positioning language

Use:

> I work on Meta acquisition testing, Shopify measurement, event-funnel diagnostics, and tracking-quality audits. I can extend the same measurement framework to GA4, GTM, CAPI, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, and e-commerce dashboards when account access is available.

Avoid unsupported statements such as “increased revenue by X%,” “fixed a 6% leak,” or “generated profitable ROAS” unless those figures are verified in a matching reporting scope.
